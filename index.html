Slide 10 , Reflection

What I’ll carry for life.

❓ What's the most important lesson about wealth building?

Start early and let compounding carry me. I don’t need to be the smartest stock picker , I need to be consistent and patient. My $50k at 12% becomes $155k in 10 years with zero extra deposits. Time does more work than my salary will at 15.

❓ How does inflation affect my future goals?

It raises the target every year. At 6% education inflation, my $25k/year today becomes $29.8k at 18 , and ~$130k total. If I earned 4% in savings, I’d lose every year. Real return is the only score that matters.

❓ Why is compounding so powerful?

Because it earns interest on interest , the curve starts flat and then goes vertical. Simple interest on $50k @12% = $110k in 10y. Compound = $155k. That $45k gap is free money for just waiting.
